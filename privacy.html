Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This is my personal website. It has no forms, no analytics, no ads and no cookies, so I collect nothing about you when you visit.

What I collect

Nothing, unless you write to me. If you email create@jonathanibrahim.com, I receive what you send and use it to reply to you. I don't sell it, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

Hosting and outside services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, to run and secure its service. Pages here also load fonts from Google Fonts, icons from Font Awesome and a script from jsDelivr, so those services see your IP address when your browser fetches their files. Their own policies cover what they do with it:

  • GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Font Awesome Privacy Policy
  • jsDelivr Privacy Policy

Links to LinkedIn or anywhere else take you off this site, and that site's own policy applies there.

JonathanOS Vault Backup

JonathanOS Vault Backup is a personal tool I registered with Google. It backs up files from my own computer to my own Google Drive. It signs in to one Google account, mine, and uses its Google Drive access only to copy files into a backup folder in that account and to restore files from it. It collects no information from anyone else. The files it copies are mine, and I don't sell them, share them or use them for advertising. Its Google sign-in stays on my computer.

JonathanOS Vault Backup's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
